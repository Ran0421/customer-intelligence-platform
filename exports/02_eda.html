Negative Churn Drivers:

  • Higher total charges
  • Long-term contracts

These findings align with earlier visual analysis and provide evidence-based business insights.

EDA Conclusion¶

Major churn drivers identified:

  1. Low customer tenure
  2. High monthly charges
  3. Month-to-month contracts
  4. Fiber optic internet service

Business Recommendations:

  • Focus retention campaigns on newly acquired customers.
  • Encourage migration to annual or two-year contracts.
  • Monitor customers with high monthly charges.
  • Improve retention initiatives for fiber optic subscribers.

These insights will guide feature engineering and predictive modeling in the next stage.

Revenue Risk Analysis¶

Beyond predicting who churns, business needs to know how much revenue is at risk. Combining monthly charges with churn probability gives a Revenue Risk Score — enabling retention teams to prioritise highest-value at-risk customers, not just highest-probability ones.

In [13]:
# Churn indicator (0/1)
df["Churn_Flag"] = (df["Churn"] == "Yes").astype(int)

# Revenue risk proxy: monthly charges of churned customers by contract type
revenue_risk = df[df["Churn_Flag"] == 1].groupby("Contract")["MonthlyCharges"].agg(
    At_Risk_Customers="count",
    Total_Monthly_Revenue_At_Risk="sum",
    Avg_Monthly_Charge="mean"
).reset_index()

revenue_risk["Total_Monthly_Revenue_At_Risk"] = revenue_risk["Total_Monthly_Revenue_At_Risk"].round(2)
revenue_risk["Avg_Monthly_Charge"] = revenue_risk["Avg_Monthly_Charge"].round(2)

print("Revenue at risk by contract type:")
revenue_risk

# Segment customers by tenure into Early / Mid / Loyal
df["Tenure_Segment"] = pd.cut(
    df["tenure"],
    bins=[0, 12, 36, 72],
    labels=["Early (0-12m)", "Mid (12-36m)", "Loyal (36m+)"]
)

churn_by_segment = df.groupby("Tenure_Segment", observed=True).agg(
    Total=("Churn_Flag", "count"),
    Churned=("Churn_Flag", "sum"),
    Monthly_Revenue_At_Risk=("MonthlyCharges", lambda x: x[df.loc[x.index, "Churn_Flag"] == 1].sum().round(2))
).reset_index()

churn_by_segment["Churn_Rate_%"] = (churn_by_segment["Churned"] / churn_by_segment["Total"] * 100).round(1)

print("Churn risk by customer tenure segment:")
churn_by_segment
